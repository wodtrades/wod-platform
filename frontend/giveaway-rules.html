1. Sponsor

This giveaway ("Giveaway") is sponsored by WOD Capital LLC, an Ohio limited liability company ("Sponsor," "WOD," "we"), reachable by opening a support ticket in our Discord. This Giveaway is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Discord Inc., or with any prop-firm partner (including Lucid Trading, Tradeify, or Alpha Futures), beyond WOD's independent business relationship with those firms.

2. Eligibility

The Giveaway is open only to individuals who, at the time of entry: (a) are at least eighteen (18) years old; (b) are legal residents of the fifty (50) United States or the District of Columbia; and (c) are not employees, contractors, or immediate family members of Sponsor. Employees or representatives of participating prop-firm partners are eligible only if they are entering as ordinary customers, not in connection with their employment. This Giveaway is void in Rhode Island and in any other jurisdiction where registration, bonding, or other formalities would be required and have not been completed by Sponsor. By entering, you represent that you meet all eligibility requirements. Sponsor reserves the right to verify eligibility and disqualify any entrant who does not meet these requirements.

3. Giveaway Period

The Giveaway runs on a recurring weekly basis. Each individual drawing period ("Entry Period") runs Saturday through the following Thursday, with winners drawn every Friday as reflected by the "Draw In" countdown displayed on the Giveaways page. Friday itself is a lock/drawing day — no new entries are added to the pool being drawn that day; activity occurring on a Friday is credited to the next Entry Period (the following Saturday through Thursday) instead. Entries do not carry over between Entry Periods. Each new Entry Period starts at zero, and eligible entrants begin earning entries again from the start of that period.

4. Free Method of Entry (No Purchase Necessary)

Every member of the WOD Discord server at discord.gg/zaVCcev9Qg automatically receives one (1) free entry into the current Entry Period at no cost and with no purchase necessary, no additional action required beyond being a verified member of the Discord. Limit one (1) free entry per person, per Entry Period, regardless of the number of Discord or social accounts used.

5. Bonus Entries (Optional Purchase)

In addition to the free entry method above, entrants may earn additional entries as follows, at no additional cost beyond the optional purchase itself:

  • Qualifying purchase: purchase an evaluation account from a participating prop-firm partner using code WOD during the active Entry Period (Saturday through Thursday), then submit the resulting order number on the Giveaways page or via the WOD Discord bot. Submitted order numbers are granted a provisional entry immediately, as described in Section 6 below. There is no limit to the number of qualifying purchases (and therefore bonus entries) an entrant may submit in a given Entry Period, subject to Section 8 below.
  • Referral bonus: share your unique referral link with a person you refer. When that person submits a qualifying order number using code WOD and that order is confirmed during Sponsor's weekly reconciliation described in Section 6, you receive one (1) additional entry for the Entry Period in which their order was placed, as described in our Referral Program. If the referred person's underlying order is later rejected during reconciliation, your associated referral entry is rejected as well. Each unique referred person may only generate one referral bonus entry, one time, ever — regardless of how many Entry Periods pass.

Bonus entries increase an entrant's relative odds of winning proportional to the total number of eligible entries received, but do not guarantee a win. Free entrants remain eligible to win the full prize on the same basis as any other entrant.

6. Winner Selection & Notification

Order numbers submitted for bonus entries under Section 5 are marked "approved" and included in the drawing pool immediately and provisionally, so that entrants are not kept waiting on a per-submission basis. This provisional approval is not a final determination that the underlying purchase is genuine. Before each Friday drawing, Sponsor cross-checks that Entry Period's submitted order numbers against affiliate records obtained directly from the participating prop-firm partners (including partner-provided export files and, where no export is available, Sponsor's own manual review of the partner's affiliate dashboard). Any order number that cannot be confirmed as a genuine, code-WOD purchase placed during the applicable Entry Period is rejected and removed from the pool prior to the drawing, along with any referral bonus entry that depended on it as described in Section 5. Provisional approval of an order number, or of any entry, is not a guarantee that the entry will remain in the pool through the drawing, and is not a guarantee of a win.

Winner(s) will be selected by random drawing from all eligible entries remaining in the pool after the reconciliation described above, within a reasonable time after the applicable Entry Period closes. Odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received. Winners will be announced in the WOD Discord server and/or contacted directly via Discord direct message using the contact information on file. A potential winner may be required to respond to notification and/or verify eligibility within five (5) business days, or the prize may be forfeited and an alternate winner selected. To claim a prize associated with a bonus entry, a winner must provide verifiable proof of the underlying qualifying purchase (such as the order confirmation from the partner firm's own portal or account dashboard) within the response window above. Sponsor reserves the right to independently confirm the order number and use of code WOD directly with the partner firm — including after a drawing has occurred — before releasing any prize. If Sponsor determines, whether before or after a drawing and whether before or after a prize has been awarded, that a submitted order number or supporting materials were fabricated, falsified, altered, or otherwise do not reflect a genuine code-WOD purchase, the associated entry is void, any resulting win is void, any prize awarded on the basis of that entry is forfeited and must be returned or its value reimbursed to Sponsor, and an alternate winner may be selected. Sponsor is not responsible for notifications lost due to inaccurate or outdated contact information, spam filters, or Discord privacy settings that block direct messages.
